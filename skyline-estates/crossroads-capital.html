How to play

Build the most celebrated city: most Legacy Points after 15 turns each (Quick: 9).

Landmark Chains

You always pursue one three-stage brief. Complete the stages in order (one stamp per action), then hold a Ribbon Cutting in your turn for +5 Legacy Points and 200 CC — +1 more if you claim it on the Skybridge. A landmark appears in the city and you pick a new brief.

The circular city

Play on one full-size circular board with the familiar Skyline property tiles, colour districts, miniature buildings and landmarks. From Start, choose the Riverside (west) or Highland (east) half of the circle. Both arcs meet at Skybridge Bonus, then the token travels through Grand Exchange in the centre and returns to Start / Payday (+200 CC once per roll). Route choices and Landmark Chase rules are unchanged.

Every landing matters

Unowned: Acquire, Reserve (25% deposit) or Auction. Yours: Open House (+50) or Upgrade. A rival’s: pay rent, then Scout new briefs or offer a deal. Metro: Express to the other line (75) or Survey (+60). Events: choose one of two City Moments. Tax and fees trade cash for Legacy Points.

Chapters and Headlines

Three chapters, each with a public City Headline: the first player to meet it wins bonus Legacy Points.

Final score

Legacy Points + 2 per complete colour group + 1 per building at House 3 or higher + 1 per 500 CC of net worth.

Rewards

Crossroads Capital pays 6× the usual match coins and Skyline Cash. In Career, Standard wins and claimed Landmarks unlock Skyline Dominion.
