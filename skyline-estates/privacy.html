Skyline Estates Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Skyline Estates is a property trading board game for iOS, Android and the web. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Offline progress is stored on your device. Optional online games, rankings and player accounts use Google Firebase and Google Cloud, as described below. The game does not include advertising or analytics features.

Online play and leaderboards

Only if you choose Play online or open the Leagues leaderboard, the app signs in anonymously with Google Firebase. It then stores on Firebase servers: the game code, the username, token and character you choose, a random player ID, and the moves and money in that game. For the season leaderboard it stores your username, level, league and trophy count for that season. Online games themselves use only the random player ID and the details above; no phone number, contacts or location are collected. Online games are visible to other players who have the code. Finished or abandoned games and old seasons may be deleted at any time. Firebase is operated by Google; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Reports and blocking

If you report a player, the report (your random player ID, their random player ID and name, the game code, the reason and the time) is stored on Firebase so it can be reviewed. Offensive usernames can be removed from leaderboards. Blocking is stored only on your device.

Player accounts and cloud save (iOS app)

A player account is optional. You can keep playing with progress saved only on your device. If you create one with email and password or Sign in with Apple, Google Firebase Authentication stores your account ID, your email address (with Sign in with Apple this may be an Apple private relay address), the sign-in methods you use and, if Apple shares it, your name. Your password is handled by Firebase and is never visible to us.

With an account, your coins, XP, collection items, equipped items and game progress flags are saved on Google Cloud servers in Belgium (europe-west1), so the same progress appears on your other devices. The first time you move your device progress to an account, a backup copy of your device save is kept on the device. A history of coin changes (earnings, spending, purchases and refunds) is kept for up to 24 months and then deleted automatically.

Removing your account

You can remove your account at any time in the app under Account & recovery → Remove account. This deletes your sign-in account, your cloud progress, the coins and items held in the account and your coin history, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Your device save stays on your device.

In-app purchases

On iPhone you can buy coin packs with Apple in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Coin packs need a player account so the coins are kept safely in your account. For each purchase we store the Apple transaction ID, the coin pack, the date, the coins credited and any refund, linked to your account, and an entry in your coin history (kept for 24 months). Coins can be spent only in the game and have no cash value.

Refunds are requested from Apple (in the app under Account & recovery → Purchases → Request a refund, or at reportaproblem.apple.com). When Apple refunds a purchase, the coins it added are removed; if they were already spent, the balance can show a debt until it is earned back. If Apple reverses a refund, the coins are returned.

If you remove your account, your coins and coin history are deleted. A record of each Apple transaction ID is kept without any link to you, only so the same purchase can never be credited twice.

What is stored on your device

Your username, saved games, settings, coins, items, token upgrades, characters, achievements and trophies, XP, season and league progress, daily missions and streaks are saved on your own device. They stay there and are removed if you delete the app or clear the site’s data.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

Online play and rankings store the usernames, random identifiers and game activity described above. Usernames are filtered for offensive words. A parent or guardian can contact support about these features.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
