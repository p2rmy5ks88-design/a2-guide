Skyline Estates Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Skyline Estates is a property trading board game for iOS, Android and the web. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Offline progress is stored on your device. Optional online games and rankings use Google Firebase and share the game information described below. The game does not include advertising or analytics features.

Online play and leaderboards

Only if you choose Play online or open the Leagues leaderboard, the app signs in anonymously with Google Firebase. It then stores on Firebase servers: the game code, the username, token and character you choose, a random player ID, and the moves and money in that game. For the season leaderboard it stores your username, level, league and trophy count for that season. No email address, phone number, contacts or location are collected. Online games are visible to other players who have the code. Finished or abandoned games and old seasons may be deleted at any time. Firebase is operated by Google; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Reports and blocking

If you report a player, the report (your random player ID, their random player ID and name, the game code, the reason and the time) is stored on Firebase so it can be reviewed. Offensive usernames can be removed from leaderboards. Blocking is stored only on your device.

Accounts and Apple sign-in testing

Online player accounts and cloud progress sync are not active in the current build. In private iOS testing, the Apple sign-in check may return an Apple identity credential, and a name or email when Apple provides them. That diagnostic does not create a Firebase player account, move coins or replace your device save. Do not send sign-in credentials or verification codes to support.

In-app purchases

Paid purchases are currently unavailable. Coin packs shown in the shop are previews and cannot be ordered. Your current coins and items are stored on your device. The daily Lucky Spin is free and spins cannot be bought. This policy will be updated before online accounts or verified store purchases are activated.

What is stored on your device

Your username, saved games, settings, coins, items, token upgrades, characters, achievements and trophies, XP, season and league progress, daily missions and streaks are saved on your own device. They stay there and are removed if you delete the app or clear the site’s data.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

Online play and rankings store the usernames, random identifiers and game activity described above. Usernames are filtered for offensive words. A parent or guardian can contact support about these features.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or online records can be sent to kelvinaiwe@gmail.com. Include your player name, game code and approximate play date where relevant; do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or identity documents. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app removes device progress, so keep your installed app while arranging recovery.
