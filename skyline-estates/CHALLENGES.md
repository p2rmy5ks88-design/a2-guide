# Web challenge extension (v1)

Existing Daily, Weekly, City Awards, Collection, 40 Season tiers and prices are preserved. Future weekly draws additionally include two qualifying Strategic or Market Pulse matches, using existing 400 Season XP, 60 Coins and SC25M weekly rewards. Existing weekly selections and done flags are kept.

Season objectives: 20 qualified games (SC50M/150 Coins/500 XP); wins in three cities (SC40M/100 Coins/350 XP/Midnight dice); 30 houses built (SC40M/100 Coins/350 XP/Chrome finish). City objectives per Season: develop three Lagos Arts Quarter properties; three Stockholm Old Town properties; own one Tokyo tower; develop both Dubai Prime Waterfront properties. Each city objective pays SC25M/60 Coins/200 XP. Lagos additionally unlocks Mint dice and Tokyo Ruby finish. Owned items use existing standard compensation, minimum150 Coins or half item price.

Only completed offline local games with recorded start, five rounds, two minutes and three recorded activities count. Only the profile owner (first human for older saves) contributes; simulations, assisted practice and online results are excluded. District targets are simultaneous final ownership/development, not purchased lifetime totals. No historical backfill is guessed.

Zero-value match receipts reuse the SC ledger; claim receipts live alongside existing profile rewards. Web Locks serialize new claims across tabs; existing reward helpers stage synchronously and one profile write commits reward plus claim. Unique SC reward receipts repair an interrupted second write on load. No lock support: progress visible, claiming disabled with explanation. Storage errors preserve existing data. Device-local timing and storage are not trusted global scoring. Season never moves backward when device clock rolls back. Deliberate local-storage tampering and a clock advanced into the future are not server-verifiable; no competitive prizes use this data.

Compact optional return overview and expandable objectives live in existing Progress/Season and Spotlight, with an earned objective list in Awards. No account or second wallet is created.
