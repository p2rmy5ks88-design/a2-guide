City Ventures — The Living Market

How to play

Build the most valuable City Value in 18 turns (Quick: 10).

Your turn

Roll, move, then buy the space you land on or send it to auction. Own 2 of a district’s 3 properties to build Low-rise and Mid-rise; own all 3 for a Landmark (+25% district rent).

The six power corners

Start — pass for 200 CC; land exactly to pick a Charter. Free Lounge — Rent Shield or keep a card. Civic Review — Development Drive (−20% upgrades) or Tenant Protection (−30% rent) for a round. Market Boom — one district’s rent +50% or −40% for a round. Skyline Exchange — open an auction or a Deal Desk trade. Transit Hub — Express Route to any station or a Transit Dividend.

Winning

City Value = cash + property values + 80% of upgrades + 200 per full district + 150 per Landmark.

Rewards

City Ventures pays 5× the usual match coins and Skyline Cash when you return to Skyline Estates. In Career, Standard wins count toward unlocking Skyline Dominion.
