How to play

Your turn

Roll and move. Land on a free street to buy it, or send it to auction. On streets you own, tap the street and upgrade it to raise its rent.

Rent and spaces

Landing on a rival's street costs rent; more upgrades mean higher rent. Passing GO pays your salary. Event spaces add or take cash, tax spaces take cash, and Civic Review skips your next turn.

Deals

Tap a rival's street to offer to buy it.

Winning

When the last round ends, the highest total value wins: cash plus streets plus what you spent on upgrades. Run out of cash and you're out.
