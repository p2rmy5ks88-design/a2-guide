Eko Estates Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Eko Estates is a property trading board game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Very little. The app does not ask for an account, has no ads and does not sell or share your data.

Online play

Only if you choose Play online, the app signs in anonymously with Google Firebase and stores the online game on Firebase servers: the game code, the player names and tokens you choose, a random player ID, and the moves and money in that game. No email, phone number, contacts or location are collected. Online games are visible to other players who have the code, and finished or abandoned games may be deleted at any time. Firebase is operated by Google; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your own device: saved games, player names you type, settings, achievements, XP, daily missions and streaks. This stays on your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact listed on the app's store page.
