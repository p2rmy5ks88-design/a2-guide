Update Skyline Estates

Keep your saved game, Skyline Cash and Coins while checking for the latest web version.

  1. Save and exit any open match.
  2. Select Check for update and wait for the download.
  3. Close every Skyline game tab and any home-screen copy, then reopen the game.

Your saved data stays on this device. This page does not clear it.
