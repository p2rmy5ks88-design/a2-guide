Rune presets

A full loadout is 13 runes: 3 Etched, 2 Blessing, 4 Enhancement and 4 Ability.

Slot rules

Etched slots
Slot 1 opens when all equipped runes are Epic, slot 2 at Legendary, slot 3 at Legendary+2. ·
Etched order
Arrow of Echoes first in every build. Griffin/Oracle: Echoes → Pulsing Orb → Orbital Orb → Elemental Crit. Dragoon: Echoes → Elemental Crit → Meteor Split → Potion Spring. ·
Fusing
3 runes of the same rarity fuse into the next tier.
Enchants
Enchantment Stones come from Shackled Jungle (clear by hand; auto-skip gives about 90% fewer) and Guild Raid's Frenzy Challenge.

Twinborn runes — free up slots

Fuse two runes of the same series into one slot, then fill the freed slot. Sharp Arrow, Arrow Rain and Revive have no partner, so they are the usual fill.

Unlock
Own any one Mythic rune.
Requires
Same type, same series, both Legendary+2 or higher, plus Runic Remnant and Twinborn Cores (40 for Enhancement/Ability, 80 for Blessing).
Undo
Twinborn Split refunds everything.
Cores
From Fishing event kg milestones (see Events → Fishing) and the Fishing Exchange Shop.
